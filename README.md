# Cold Outreach Literature Review & Playbook

Research and a sourced operator playbook for B2B SaaS cold outreach, built from ten cold-email practitioners' public content (YouTube, LinkedIn, interviews, and other long-form material).

## Contents

- **[PLAYBOOK.md](PLAYBOOK.md)** — the finished deliverable. A full Cold Outreach Playbook / SOP, built only from claims with a real link, real date, and verbatim quote. It contains:
  - The operator checklist itself (sections 1–7: list building, deliverability, sequence structure, copywriting, personalization, follow-ups, metrics), every line cited `(source: Author, link from DD.MM.YYYY)`.
  - **Where experts disagree** — the genuine conflicts in the sourced material, with reasoning for each: two left unresolved, one resolved with a defended judgment call.
  - **What I rejected and why** — sourced recommendations that were deliberately left out of the SOP, with reasoning.
  - **Who I would NOT recommend following (and why)** — an assessment of which of the ten sources was weakest for building a citable playbook.
  - **Weaknesses of this playbook** — an honest accounting of the limitations in the evidence base and the synthesis itself.
  - Working notes (conflicts log, coverage gaps) kept at the end for process transparency.

  Not included: an "original ideas" section — this playbook is a synthesis of sourced expert recommendations plus judgment calls on how to reconcile them, not a vehicle for untested ideas of my own.

- **[research/](research/)** — the underlying research.
  - `research/sources.md` — the ten experts referenced, with links, dates, and brief annotations.
  - `research/claims-table.md` — every extractable claim from the research, one row per claim, with author, source link, date, the SOP section it maps to, and a verbatim anchor quote. Includes an "Unsourced or undated" appendix for claims that didn't clear the citation bar.
  - `research/youtube-transcripts/`, `research/linkedin-posts/`, `research/other/` — raw source material, organized by author.

## Sourcing policy

A claim only makes it into the claims table (and from there, the playbook) if the source file gives a real, resolvable per-post/per-video link, a full precise date, and a genuine verbatim quote — not a note-taker's paraphrase. Claims that fail any of those checks are logged separately rather than silently dropped.
